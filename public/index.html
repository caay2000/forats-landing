Members

Book without an account

Join your organization's space with a code and start booking instantly.

  • Join with a code — no account needed
  • Color-coded availability calendar — green, yellow and red at a glance
  • Book with name, email and phone — details pre-filled from last time
  • Your bookings shown as "Your booking" — never confused with others
  • Upcoming bookings visible right on the room list
  • Full booking history — upcoming and past in one place
  • Cancel any upcoming booking instantly

Admin

Full control of your space

Create the space, manage rooms, and view all bookings with full contact details.

  • Create your space in under 3 minutes
  • Add and manage as many rooms as you need
  • Weekly schedule per room — different hours each day, close specific days
  • See all bookings with full contact details
  • Block time slots — overlapping member bookings cancelled automatically
  • Cancel any booking from the room detail
  • Disable a room and batch-cancel all future bookings in one tap
  • Share the space code via WhatsApp, email or any platform
  • Access your space from any device with code + password
  • Edit space name, address and hours any time
  • Configure booking rules: min/max duration, notice, cancellation window and per-member quotas
  • Add your space's logo to personalize the experience

Backoffice

Manage your space from a computer too

The same admin panel, now in your browser — nothing to install.

  • Sign in with the same space code and password
  • Manage rooms, schedules and bookings from any computer
  • Handy for the front desk or working from home
